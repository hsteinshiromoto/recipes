# Chicken Schnitzel Salad with Yoghurt Dressing

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Schnitzel]] - 400g (pre-made, sliced)
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 100g (fat-free)
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 1 clove, minced
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, sliced
- [ ] [[Mixed Salad Leaves]] - 150g
- [ ] [[Dill]] - 1 tablespoon, chopped
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions
1. Warm the [[Chicken Schnitzel]] in a dry non-stick pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes per side until heated through and crispy.
2. Slice the schnitzels into strips.
3. Make the dressing: whisk together [[Greek Yoghurt]], [[Lemon]] juice, minced [[Garlic]], [[Dill]], salt, and pepper.
4. In a large [[Bowl]], combine [[Mixed Salad Leaves]], diced [[Cucumber]], diced [[Tomato]], and sliced [[Capsicum]].
5. Top with the sliced chicken schnitzel.
6. Drizzle with yoghurt dressing and serve.
