# Lean Beef and Vegetable Stir Fry

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Flank Steak]] - 400g, sliced thin against the grain
- [ ] [[Broccoli]] - 200g, cut into florets
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, sliced
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 1, sliced thin on the bias
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Oyster Sauce]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Rice]] - to serve

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a [[wok]] over high heat until smoking. Add 1 teaspoon of oil.
2. Sear the [[Flank Steak]] slices in batches for 1-2 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
3. Add the [[Broccoli]], [[Capsicum]], and [[Carrot]] to the [[wok]]. Stir fry for 3-4 minutes.
4. Add the [[Garlic]] and [[Ginger]], cook for 30 seconds.
5. Return the beef to the [[wok]].
6. Add [[light soy sauce]] and [[Oyster Sauce]], toss everything together for 1 minute.
7. Garnish with [[Sesame Seeds]].
8. Serve over steamed [[Rice]].
