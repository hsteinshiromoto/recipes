# Lemon Garlic Prawns with Asparagus

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Prawns]] - 500g, peeled and deveined
- [ ] [[Asparagus]] - 300g, trimmed and cut into 4cm pieces
- [ ] [[Cherry Tomatoes]] - 150g, halved
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 4 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - 1/4 tsp
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Quinoa]] - 200g, cooked (use batch-prepped from Saturday)
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Wok]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
2. Add asparagus pieces and cook for 3 minutes until bright green and tender-crisp.
3. Add garlic and chilli flakes, cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add prawns and cook for 2 minutes per side until pink and just cooked through.
5. Add cherry tomatoes and toss for 1 minute until slightly softened.
6. Remove from heat. Squeeze lemon juice over and scatter with lemon zest.
7. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with fresh parsley.
8. Serve over cooked quinoa.
