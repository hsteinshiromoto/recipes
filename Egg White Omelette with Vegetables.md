# Egg White Omelette with Vegetables

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Egg]] - 12 whites (or 6 whole eggs plus 6 whites)
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2, diced
- [ ] [[Spinach]] - 80g
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Fetta]] - 30g, crumbled
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat with a light spray of oil.
2. Saute the diced [[Onion]] and [[Capsicum]] for 2 minutes.
3. Add the [[Spinach]] and cook until wilted, about 1 minute.
4. Add the diced [[Tomato]] and stir briefly.
5. Pour in the whisked egg whites (or whole eggs plus whites mix).
6. Cook without stirring until the edges set, about 2-3 minutes.
7. Fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute.
8. Top with crumbled [[Fetta]].
9. Divide into 3 portions and serve.
