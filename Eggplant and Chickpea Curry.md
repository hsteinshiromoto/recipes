# Eggplant and Chickpea Curry

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Eggplant]] - 2 medium, cut into 2cm cubes (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Chickpeas]] - 2 x 400g cans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 400g canned diced tomatoes
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1 large, finely diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 4 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Coriander Seed]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Turmeric]] - 1/2 tsp
- [ ] [[Garam Masala]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1/2 tsp
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - 1/4 tsp
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 2 tbsp fat-free, for serving
- [ ] [[Cilantro]] - small handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tbsp
- [ ] Salt to taste

## Equipment

- [[Wok]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large deep pan over medium-high heat.
2. Add eggplant cubes and cook for 5-6 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned. Remove and set aside.
3. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion and cook for 3 minutes until softened.
4. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute.
5. Add cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, paprika, and chilli flakes. Stir for 30 seconds.
6. Add canned tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes until sauce thickens slightly.
7. Add chickpeas and return eggplant to the pan.
8. Simmer for 15 minutes until eggplant is tender and flavours have melded.
9. Stir in lemon juice and season with salt.
10. Serve topped with a dollop of Greek yoghurt and fresh cilantro.
    - Serve with steamed rice or naan. Makes 4 portions; save 1 extra for next day lunch.
