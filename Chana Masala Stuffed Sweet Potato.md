# Chana Masala Stuffed Sweet Potato

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Sweet Potato]] - 3 medium (approx 200g each), scrubbed (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Chickpeas]] - 1 x 400g can, drained and rinsed
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 1 x 400g can diced tomatoes
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1 small, finely diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 1cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Coriander Seed]] - 1/2 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Turmeric]] - 1/4 tsp
- [ ] [[Garam Masala]] - 1/2 tsp
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1/2 tsp
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - pinch
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 3 tbsp fat-free
- [ ] [[Cilantro]] - handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1/2, juiced
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Oven]]
- [[Baking tray]]
- [[Wok]]

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200C. Pierce each sweet potato several times with a fork. Place on a baking tray and bake for 35-40 minutes until completely tender.
2. While sweet potatoes bake, heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook onion for 3 minutes.
3. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, paprika, and chilli flakes. Stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add chickpeas and canned tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
5. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
6. When sweet potatoes are ready, split them open lengthwise and gently push down the flesh.
7. Spoon chickpea masala generously over each potato.
8. Top with Greek yoghurt and fresh cilantro.
