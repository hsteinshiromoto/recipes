# Prawn and Zucchini Noodle Bowl

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Prawns]] - 400g, peeled and deveined
- [ ] [[Zucchini]] - 3 large, spiralised or julienned
- [ ] [[Cherry Tomatoes]] - 150g, halved
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - 1/2 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Parmiggiano]] - 20g, grated (optional)
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a large pan or [[wok]] over medium-high heat with 1 teaspoon of olive oil.
2. Add the [[Prawns]] and cook for 2 minutes per side until pink. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add [[Garlic]] and [[Chilli Flakes]], cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add the spiralised [[Zucchini]] and [[Cherry Tomatoes]]. Toss for 2-3 minutes until zucchini is just tender.
5. Return the prawns to the pan.
6. Add [[Lemon]] juice and zest, toss to combine.
7. Garnish with [[Parsley]] and a light grating of [[Parmiggiano]].
8. Serve immediately.
