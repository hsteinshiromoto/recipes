# Spicy Prawn and Vegetable Stir Fry

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Prawns]] - 400g, peeled and deveined
- [ ] [[Broccoli]] - 200g, cut into small florets
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, sliced
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 3, sliced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - 1/2 teaspoon
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Fish Sauce]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Lime]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Rice]] - to serve

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a [[wok]] over high heat with 1 teaspoon of oil.
2. Add the [[Prawns]] and cook for 2 minutes per side until pink. Remove and set aside.
3. Add [[Broccoli]] and [[Capsicum]], stir fry for 3 minutes.
4. Add [[Garlic]], [[Ginger]], and [[Chilli Flakes]], cook for 30 seconds.
5. Return the prawns to the [[wok]].
6. Add [[light soy sauce]] and [[Fish Sauce]], toss to combine.
7. Squeeze [[Lime]] juice over and garnish with [[scallion]].
8. Serve over steamed [[Rice]].
