# Tuna and White Bean Salad

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Tuna]] - 300g (2 cans in spring water, drained)
- [ ] [[White Beans]] - 400g (1 can, drained and rinsed)
- [ ] [[Arugula]] - 100g
- [ ] [[Cherry Tomatoes]] - 200g, halved
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2 red, thinly sliced
- [ ] [[Celery]] - 2 stalks, sliced
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Dijon mustard]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions
1. In a large [[Bowl]], combine the drained [[Tuna]], drained [[White Beans]], [[Arugula]], halved [[Cherry Tomatoes]], sliced [[Onion]], and sliced [[Celery]].
2. In a small bowl, whisk together [[Lemon]] juice, [[Dijon mustard]], salt, and pepper.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently to combine.
4. Fold in the chopped [[Parsley]].
5. Serve immediately or refrigerate for up to 2 days.
