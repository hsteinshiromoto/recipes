# Turkey Lettuce Wraps

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Turkey Mince]] - 500g
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Rice Vinegar]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Hoisin]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Sriracha]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 1, finely diced
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, finely diced
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 3, sliced
- [ ] [[Lettuce]] - 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a [[wok]] over high heat with 1 teaspoon of oil.
2. Add the [[Turkey Mince]] and cook, breaking it up, for 5-6 minutes until browned.
3. Add the [[Garlic]], [[Ginger]], diced [[Carrot]], and [[Capsicum]]. Stir fry for 3 minutes.
4. Add [[light soy sauce]], [[Rice Vinegar]], [[Hoisin]], and [[Sriracha]]. Toss to combine and cook 1 minute.
5. Garnish with sliced [[scallion]].
6. Serve in [[Lettuce]] cups.
