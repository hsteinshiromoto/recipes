# Black Bean and Chicken Burrito Bowl

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 400g, diced
- [ ] [[Black Beans]] - 400g (1 can, drained and rinsed)
- [ ] [[Rice]] - 200g
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1/2 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Lime]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Cilantro]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 100g (fat-free, as sour cream substitute)
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Instant Pot]]

## Instructions
1. Cook the [[Rice]] in the [[Instant Pot]] (3 minutes high pressure for basmati).
2. While rice cooks, heat a large pan over medium-high heat with 1 teaspoon of oil.
3. Season the diced [[Chicken Breast]] with [[Cumin]], [[Smoked Paprika]], salt, and pepper. Cook for 6-7 minutes until golden.
4. Add the diced [[Onion]], [[Capsicum]], and [[Garlic]]. Cook for 3 minutes.
5. Add the [[Black Beans]] and diced [[Tomato]]. Stir and cook for 3 minutes until heated through.
6. Squeeze [[Lime]] juice over the mixture.
7. Serve over rice, topped with [[Cilantro]] and a dollop of [[Greek Yoghurt]].
