# Poached Chicken and Rice Soup

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 500g
- [ ] [[Rice]] - 100g
- [ ] [[Chicken Broth]] - 1.5L
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Celery]] - 2 stalks, diced
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, sliced
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Instant Pot]]

## Instructions
1. In a large pot, bring the [[Chicken Broth]] to a simmer.
2. Add the whole [[Chicken Breast]], [[Ginger]], and [[Garlic]]. Poach for 15 minutes until cooked through.
3. Remove the chicken and shred with two forks.
4. Add the diced [[Onion]], [[Carrot]], [[Celery]], and [[Rice]] to the broth.
5. Cook for 10 minutes until rice and vegetables are tender.
6. Return the shredded chicken to the soup.
7. Season with [[Lemon]] juice, salt, and pepper.
8. Garnish with [[Parsley]].
