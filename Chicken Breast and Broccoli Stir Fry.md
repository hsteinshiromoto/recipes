# Chicken Breast and Broccoli Stir Fry

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 500g, sliced thin
- [ ] [[Broccoli]] - 300g, cut into florets
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 4 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Oyster Sauce]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 2, sliced
- [ ] [[Rice]] - to serve

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Season the sliced [[Chicken Breast]] with a pinch of salt.
2. Heat a [[wok]] over high heat with 1 teaspoon of oil.
3. Add the chicken and stir fry for 4-5 minutes until golden. Remove and set aside.
4. In the same [[wok]], add the [[Broccoli]] with 2 tablespoons of water. Cover and steam for 2 minutes.
5. Add the [[Garlic]] and [[Ginger]], stir fry for 30 seconds.
6. Return the chicken to the [[wok]].
7. Add [[light soy sauce]] and [[Oyster Sauce]], toss to combine.
8. Garnish with [[Sesame Seeds]] and sliced [[scallion]].
9. Serve over steamed [[Rice]].
