# Chickpea and Tuna Salad

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chickpeas]] - 400g (1 can, drained and rinsed)
- [ ] [[Tuna]] - 300g (2 cans in spring water, drained)
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2 red, finely diced
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Dijon mustard]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions
1. In a large [[Bowl]], combine the drained [[Chickpeas]], drained [[Tuna]], diced [[Cucumber]], diced [[Tomato]], and diced [[Onion]].
2. In a small bowl, whisk together [[Lemon]] juice, [[Dijon mustard]], salt, and pepper.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
4. Fold in the chopped [[Parsley]].
5. Serve immediately or refrigerate for up to 2 days.
