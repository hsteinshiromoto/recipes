# Cottage Cheese Pancakes

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Cottage Cheese]] - 300g (low-fat)
- [ ] [[Egg]] - 4
- [ ] [[Rolled Oats]] - 80g (blended into flour)
- [ ] [[Banana]] - 1, mashed
- [ ] [[Vanilla Extract]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Blueberries]] - 100g (for topping)
- [ ] [[Honey]] - 1 tablespoon (for topping)

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. In a blender, combine [[Cottage Cheese]], [[Egg|eggs]], blended [[Rolled Oats]], mashed [[Banana]], and [[Vanilla Extract]].
2. Blend until smooth.
3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
4. Pour about 60ml of batter per pancake.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface, then flip.
6. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden.
7. Serve topped with [[Blueberries]] and a drizzle of [[Honey]].
