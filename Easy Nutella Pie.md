## **Key Ingredients**

1.  **[Heavy whipping cream](https://amzn.to/3EKQ3nK):** Make sure it has at least 35% fat content. We’ll whip it until soft peaks form to give the filling that light, airy texture.
2.  **[Cream cheese](https://amzn.to/3Fn1VMP):** Let it come to room temperature before using so it mixes smoothly into the filling without any lumps.
3.  **[Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) (or any chocolate hazelnut spread):** We’ll use it for the rich, creamy filling and a little extra for decorating on top.
4.  **Melted [butter](https://amzn.to/4iRLl5n):** Mixed with the Oreo crumbs to bind the crust together and give it that perfect firm, yet buttery bite.
5.  **[Oreos](https://amzn.to/4d9hJ26):** Crush into fine crumbs—these form the base of our crust.

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9496.webp)

This recipe makes about 8 servings, but feel free to cut smaller or larger slices depending on how many people you’re serving. Need to feed a crowd? Just double (or even triple!) the recipe to make sure there’s plenty of this creamy [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) goodness to go around.

## **How to prepare this Nutella pie**

Start by crushing the [Oreos](https://amzn.to/4d9hJ26) into fine crumbs using a [food processor](https://amzn.to/3ZeEyvx). Mix the crumbs with melted [butter](https://amzn.to/4iRLl5n) until the mixture resembles wet sand.

Pour the crust mixture into your [springform pan](https://amzn.to/4kbVNpk), pressing it firmly along the sides first, then into the bottom to create an even base. Place the crust in the freezer while you prepare the filling—this helps it set quickly.

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9488.webp)

In a mixing bowl, combine the room-temperature [cream cheese](https://amzn.to/3Fn1VMP) and [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc). Use a [spatula](https://amzn.to/4iZVT2h) or wooden spoon—**avoid using a [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S)**, as it can change the texture and prevent the filling from setting properly.

In a separate, _completely dry and clean_ bowl, whip the [heavy whipping cream](https://amzn.to/3EKQ3nK) until soft peaks form. This is key: if there’s any moisture or residue in the bowl or on the [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S), the cream won’t whip correctly.

Once whipped, gently fold the [whipped cream](https://amzn.to/4iXeCvu) into the [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) mixture using a [spatula](https://amzn.to/4iZVT2h).

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9483.webp)

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9482.webp)

Pour the filling over your prepared crust and smooth the top. Give the pan a few gentle taps on the counter to release any air bubbles and level the surface.

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9489.webp)

Place the pie in the freezer for **4 hours** to set, or in the refrigerator for at least **6 hours** if you prefer a softer texture. Once set, drizzle melted [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) on top and tilt the pan to spread it evenly for a glossy, delicious finish.

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9493.webp)

![](https://archersfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img_9494.webp)

Slice, serve, and enjoy every creamy bite!

## Best Tips to Make a Nutella Pie

1.  **No [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S) for filling:** Use a [spatula](https://amzn.to/4iZVT2h) or wooden spoon to mix [cream cheese](https://amzn.to/3Fn1VMP) and [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc)—do not use a [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S), or the texture may change and the filling won’t set properly.
2.  **Clean and dry whipping tools:** When whipping cream, the bowl and [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S) must be completely dry and clean or the cream won’t whip.
3.  **Soft peaks only:** Whip the heavy cream until soft peaks form—not stiff—to keep the filling light and airy.
4.  **Set time (freezer or fridge):** Freeze for 4 hours for a firmer pie or refrigerate for at least 6 hours for a softer set.
5.  **Use [heavy whipping cream](https://amzn.to/3EKQ3nK) with at least 35% fat content:** this ensures it whips up nicely into soft peaks and gives your [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) pie filling that rich, creamy texture everyone love.

FAQs

Can I use a different chocolate spread instead of [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc)?

Yes! While [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) is classic, any chocolate hazelnut spread will work great in this recipe for that rich, creamy flavor.

Do I have to bake this [Nutella](https://amzn.to/4m8tsCc) pie?

Nope! This is a no-bake dessert, so there’s no oven required. Just chill or freeze the pie to set the filling perfectly.

How do I know when the heavy cream is whipped enough?

You want to whip the heavy cream until it holds soft peaks—that means when you lift the [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S), the cream forms gentle peaks that slightly droop.

Why is my filling not setting properly?

If your filling won’t set, it could be because the [cream cheese](https://amzn.to/3Fn1VMP) wasn’t at room temperature or you used a [whisk](https://amzn.to/4m7d58S) instead of folding with a [spatula](https://amzn.to/4iZVT2h), which affects texture.
