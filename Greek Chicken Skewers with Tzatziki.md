# Greek Chicken Skewers with Tzatziki

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 500g, cut into 2cm cubes
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[oregano]] - 1 tablespoon dried
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1/2 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 200g (fat-free)
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, half grated and half sliced
- [ ] [[Dill]] - 2 tablespoons, chopped
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2, cut into wedges
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2 red, sliced
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Baking tray]]

## Instructions
### Chicken
1. Combine [[Chicken Breast]] cubes with half the [[Lemon]] juice, [[Garlic]], [[oregano]], [[Cumin]], 1 tablespoon [[Greek Yoghurt]], salt, and pepper.
2. Thread onto skewers.
3. Grill on a hot pan or grill for 5-6 minutes per side until charred and cooked through (74C internal).

### Tzatziki
1. Grate half the [[Cucumber]] and squeeze out excess moisture.
2. Combine with remaining [[Greek Yoghurt]], remaining [[Lemon]] juice, 1 clove minced [[Garlic]], [[Dill]], salt, and pepper.

### To Serve
1. Serve skewers with tzatziki, sliced [[Cucumber]], [[Tomato]] wedges, and [[Onion]] slices.
