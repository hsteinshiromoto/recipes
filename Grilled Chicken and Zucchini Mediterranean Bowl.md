# Grilled Chicken and Zucchini Mediterranean Bowl

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 500g, butterflied
- [ ] [[Zucchini]] - 2 medium, sliced lengthwise into 1cm planks (late summer seasonal)
- [ ] [[Cherry Tomatoes]] - 200g, halved
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Arugula]] - 100g
- [ ] [[Fetta]] - 40g low-fat, crumbled
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[oregano]] - 1 tsp dried
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2 red, thinly sliced
- [ ] [[Quinoa]] - 200g, cooked
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Baking tray]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Season the butterflied chicken breasts with half the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Let rest for 5 minutes.
2. Heat a grill pan over medium-high heat with 1 tsp olive oil. Cook chicken for 5-6 minutes per side until charred and cooked through (internal temp 74C). Set aside to rest for 3 minutes.
3. In the same pan, grill zucchini planks for 2 minutes per side until charred and tender.
4. Cook quinoa according to packet directions (or use batch-prepped quinoa).
5. Slice the rested chicken into strips.
6. Assemble bowls: quinoa base, topped with arugula, grilled zucchini, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
7. Place sliced chicken on top. Crumble fetta over.
8. Drizzle with remaining lemon juice and zest. Season and serve.
