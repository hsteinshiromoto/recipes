# Espresso Brownies

The recipe card has no title; named after its key ingredient.

![[Espresso Brownies.jpg]]

## Ingredients
- [ ] 115 g (4 oz) unsalted [[butter]]
- [ ] 200 g (1 cup) sugar
- [ ] 2.5 mL (1/4 tsp) [[Vanilla Extract|vanilla extract]]
- [ ] 60 mL (1 shot / 4 tbsp) freshly brewed espresso or very strong [[Coffee|coffee]]
- [ ] 2 large (100 g) [[Egg|eggs]]
- [ ] 80 g (1/2 cup) all-purpose flour
- [ ] 80 g (1/3 cup) Hershey's [[Cocoa Powder|cocoa powder]]
- [ ] 1.3 g (1/4 tsp) [[baking soda]]
- [ ] 1.5 g (1/4 tsp) table salt

## Equipment
- [[oven]]
- 8×8 in pan

## Instructions
1. Butter and flour an 8×8 in pan.
2. Preheat the oven to 170°C (350°F).
3. Melt the butter, then mix in the sugar, vanilla and espresso.
4. Mix in the eggs.
5. Fold in the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda and salt.
6. Bake at 170°C (350°F) for 30 to 40 min.
