# Greek Yoghurt Marinated Chicken with Vegetables

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 500g, butterflied
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 150g (fat-free)
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Turmeric]] - 1/2 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Zucchini]] - 2, sliced into half-moons
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, cut into chunks
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1, cut into wedges
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Baking tray]]
- [[oven]]

## Instructions
1. In a [[Bowl]], combine [[Greek Yoghurt]], [[Lemon]] juice and zest, [[Garlic]], [[Cumin]], [[Smoked Paprika]], [[Turmeric]], salt, and pepper.
2. Add the butterflied [[Chicken Breast]] and coat thoroughly. If time permits, marinate for 30 minutes or overnight.
3. Preheat [[oven]] to 220C (425F) or heat a grill pan over medium-high heat.
4. Remove chicken from marinade (reserve excess marinade for basting).
5. Cook chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until charred and cooked through (74C internal).
6. Meanwhile, toss [[Zucchini]], [[Capsicum]], and [[Onion]] with a light spray of oil, salt, and pepper. Grill or roast alongside chicken for 10 minutes.
7. Let chicken rest for 3 minutes, then slice.
8. Serve sliced chicken over vegetables, garnished with [[Parsley]].
