# Teriyaki Salmon Bowl with Edamame

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Salmon Fillets]] - 3 fillets, approx 150g each
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 3 tbsp (low-sodium)
- [ ] [[Honey]] - 1.5 tbsp
- [ ] [[Rice Vinegar]] - 1 tbsp
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 1cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[Rice]] - 300g dry, cooked as per packet (to make extra for reuse)
- [ ] [[Edamame]] - 200g frozen, thawed and shelled
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, thinly sliced
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 1, peeled and julienned
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tbsp
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 3 stalks, sliced thinly

## Equipment

- [[Wok]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Cook rice according to packet directions. Cook 300g dry rice to yield a large batch (extra for reuse next day).
2. Mix soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, garlic, and ginger to make teriyaki sauce.
3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Place salmon fillets skin-side down and cook for 4 minutes.
4. Flip salmon. Spoon half the teriyaki sauce over the salmon and cook for another 3 minutes until just cooked through.
5. Meanwhile, arrange steamed rice, edamame, cucumber, and carrot in bowls.
6. Place salmon on top. Drizzle with remaining teriyaki sauce.
7. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced scallions.
