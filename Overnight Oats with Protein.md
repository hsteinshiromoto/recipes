# Overnight Oats with Protein

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Rolled Oats]] - 180g
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 300g (fat-free)
- [ ] [[Milk]] - 250ml (skim)
- [ ] [[Honey]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Chia Seeds]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Banana]] - 1, sliced
- [ ] [[Blueberries]] - 100g

## Equipment
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions
1. In a large [[Bowl]], combine the [[Rolled Oats]], [[Greek Yoghurt]], [[Milk]], [[Honey]], and [[Chia Seeds]].
2. Stir until well mixed.
3. Divide into 3 jars or containers.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours).
5. Before serving, top with sliced [[Banana]] and [[Blueberries]].
