# Mushroom and Leek Frittata

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Egg]] - 8 large eggs
- [ ] [[Mushroom]] - 200g button or Swiss brown mushrooms, sliced (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Leek]] - 1 large leek, white and pale green parts, sliced (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Fetta]] - 60g low-fat, crumbled
- [ ] [[Spinach]] - 60g baby spinach
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - 2 tbsp, chopped
- [ ] [[Milk]] - 2 tbsp skim milk
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Baking tray]]
- [[Oven]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 180C.
2. Whisk eggs with skim milk, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe non-stick pan (26cm) over medium heat. Add leek and cook for 3 minutes until softened.
4. Add garlic and mushrooms. Cook for 4 minutes until mushrooms are golden and any liquid has evaporated.
5. Add spinach and stir until wilted, about 1 minute.
6. Pour the egg mixture evenly over the vegetables.
7. Scatter crumbled fetta on top.
8. Cook on stovetop for 2 minutes until edges begin to set.
9. Transfer pan to oven and bake for 10-12 minutes until frittata is set through.
10. Remove from oven, let cool for 2 minutes. Scatter with fresh parsley. Slice into wedges to serve.
    - Makes 4 portions; serves 3 today, pack 1 for tomorrow's snack/breakfast.
