# Cumin Lamb

Xinjiang-style wok-seared [[Lamb Leg Steaks|lamb]], cubed small and marinated in egg for a silky coating, seared hard, then tossed with garlic, red onion, spring onion and toasted cumin seeds and chilli flakes.

## Ingredients
- [ ] 400g [[Lamb Leg Steaks|lamb]], cut into 1–2cm cubes
- [ ] 1 [[Egg]], beaten
- [ ] 2 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- [ ] 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- [ ] 2–3 spring onions, cut into batons
- [ ] 1 tbsp [[Cumin]] seeds
- [ ] 1 tsp chilli flakes
- [ ] Sesame seeds, for garnish
- [ ] Neutral cooking oil
- [ ] Salt, to taste

## Equipment
- [ ] [[wok]]

## Instructions

1. Cut the lamb into small cubes, about 1 to 2cm across.
2. Mix the lamb in one direction with the beaten egg until evenly coated.
3. In a dry wok, toast the cumin seeds and chilli flakes briefly until fragrant, then set aside.
4. Heat oil in the wok over high heat.
5. Sear the lamb for about 2 minutes undisturbed to develop colour.
6. Add the minced garlic and stir through.
7. Turn the heat up to high, add the red onion and spring onion, and toss.
8. Add back the toasted cumin seeds and chilli flakes (use fewer chilli flakes for a milder dish).
9. Give everything a final toss, season with salt to taste, and finish with sesame seeds.
10. Serve immediately — the lamb should be tender and juicy on the inside.
