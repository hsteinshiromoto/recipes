# Flan Crème Caramel

Scaled for the [[Nordic Ware Formed Tube Cake Pan, 12 Cup]]. Original ChefSteps recipe uses sous vide jars; adapted here for oven bain-marie. Scale factor: **1.4×** the original base recipe.

## Ingredients

### Caramel

| Ingredient | Original (9 serves) | Scaled 1.4× (12-cup pan) |
|---|---|---|
| [[Caster sugar]] | 350 g | 500 g |
| Water | 50 g | 70 g |

### Custard

| Ingredient | Original (9 serves) | Scaled 1.4× (12-cup pan) |
|---|---|---|
| [[Egg\|Whole eggs]] | 250 g (~5 large eggs) | 350 g (~7 large eggs) |
| [[Caster sugar]] | 250 g | 350 g |
| [[Salt]] | 4 g | 6 g |
| [[Egg\|Egg yolks]] | 125 g (~7 yolks) | 175 g (~10 yolks) |
| [[Milk\|Whole milk]] | 750 g | 1,050 g |
| [[Heavy Cream]] | 500 g | 700 g |

## Equipment

- [[Nordic Ware Formed Tube Cake Pan, 12 Cup]]
- Large roasting pan (for bain-marie)
- Candy thermometer (optional)
- [[Strainer|Fine-mesh strainer]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

### Caramel
1. Preheat oven to 163°C (325°F).
2. Combine 500 g sugar and 70 g water in a heavy saucepan. Heat over medium-high without stirring until the caramel reaches a deep amber colour (~160°C / 320°F).
3. Immediately pour the hot caramel into the Bundt pan. Tilt and rotate quickly to coat the bottom and partway up the sides. Set aside to harden.

### Custard
4. Whisk together the whole eggs, egg yolks, 350 g sugar, and salt in a large bowl until smooth.
5. Warm the milk and heavy cream in a saucepan over medium heat until lightly steaming (~80°C / 176°F). Do not boil.
6. Slowly pour the hot milk mixture into the egg mixture in a thin stream, whisking constantly to temper the eggs.
7. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a large jug. Let rest 20–30 minutes to allow bubbles to dissipate; skim any remaining foam.
8. Gently pour the custard into the caramel-coated Bundt pan.

### Option A — Bain-Marie (oven)
9. Place the Bundt pan inside a large roasting pan. Transfer to the oven, then carefully pour enough hot water into the roasting pan to reach halfway up the outside of the Bundt pan.
10. Bake at 163°C (325°F) for 60–75 minutes, until the custard is just set at the edges but still jiggles softly in the centre when nudged.
11. Remove the Bundt pan from the water bath and cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours (overnight preferred).

### Option B — Sous Vide (individual jars)
> Note: a 12-cup Bundt pan is too large for a standard sous vide bath. Use wide-mouth 240 ml (8 oz) canning jars instead — the scaled recipe fills approximately 13 jars.

9. Set the immersion circulator to 90°C (194°F).
10. Coat the inside of each jar with a thin layer of caramel and let it harden.
11. Fill each jar with custard, leaving ~1 cm (¼ inch) of headspace.
12. Seal each jar fingertip-tight (do not over-tighten — air must escape during cooking).
13. Submerge jars in the water bath and cook for 1 hour.
14. Check doneness by gently shaking a jar — the custard should jiggle as a single mass, not flow.
15. Transfer jars to an ice-water bath to chill, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
16. To serve, run a knife around the inside edge of each jar and invert onto a plate.

### Unmoulding
12. Run a thin knife around the inner and outer edges of the pan to loosen.
13. Place a large serving plate over the pan and invert quickly. The caramel will flow over as a sauce.
14. Serve chilled, optionally garnished with fresh fruit or mint.

## Notes

- The caramel hardens fast — have the Bundt pan ready before you start cooking the sugar.
- Resting and skimming the custard before baking prevents air bubbles and gives a smooth surface.
- The custard is done when it jiggles as a single mass, not when it flows like liquid.
