# Rice Cooker Bibimbap

A "cheats" one-pot bibimbap: marinated beef and vegetables cook on top of the rice in the rice cooker, then it's all mixed together with gochujang and topped with a fried egg. The bottom crisps up like in a dolsot.

The video gives no quantities. These are reasonable amounts for 2 serves; adjust to taste.

![[Rice Cooker Bibimbap.jpg]]

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup (200 g) short-grain [[Rice|rice]], rinsed
- [ ] Water to the rice cooker's line for that much rice
- [ ] 1 tbsp [[sesame oil]] (or neutral oil), for a crispy base
- [ ] 250 g thinly sliced beef (e.g. [[Flank Steak|flank steak]])
- [ ] 1–2 tbsp sweet & spicy Korean BBQ seasoning (or a bulgogi marinade)
- [ ] A handful of sliced [[Shitake|shiitake mushrooms]]
- [ ] A handful of baby [[Spinach|spinach]]
- [ ] 1 [[Carrot|carrot]], julienned
- [ ] 1/2 [[Cucumber|cucumber]], julienned
- [ ] A handful of [[bean sprouts]]
- [ ] 2 [[Egg|eggs]], fried
- [ ] 2 tbsp [[Gochujang|gochujang]] sauce

### To serve
- [ ] Sliced [[scallion|spring onion]]
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds|Sesame seeds]]
- [ ] [[Kimchi]]
- [ ] Hot sauce (optional)

## Equipment
- [[Rice Cooker]]

## Instructions

1. Season the sliced beef with the Korean BBQ seasoning and mix well.
2. Add the rinsed rice and water to the rice cooker, then drizzle in the oil.
3. Spread the marinated beef over the rice.
4. Top with the mushrooms, spinach, carrot, cucumber and bean sprouts.
5. Close the lid and cook on the normal rice setting.
6. When it's done, add a fried egg and the gochujang sauce, then mix everything together, scraping up the crispy rice from the bottom.
7. Serve in bowls topped with another fried egg, spring onion, sesame seeds, kimchi and hot sauce.
