# Spiced Lamb and Cauliflower Tray Bake

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Lamb Leg Steaks]] - 450g, trimmed of visible fat
- [ ] [[Cauliflower]] - 1 small head, cut into florets (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Pumpkin]] - 250g, peeled and cubed (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 4 cloves, whole
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Coriander Seed]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Cinnamon]] - 1/4 tsp
- [ ] [[Turmeric]] - 1/4 tsp
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Olive oil]] - 1 tbsp
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - handful, chopped
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Baking tray]]
- [[Oven]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 210C.
2. In a bowl, combine cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon, turmeric, half the lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
3. Rub two-thirds of the spice mixture over the lamb steaks. Set aside.
4. Toss cauliflower, pumpkin, onion wedges, and garlic cloves with the remaining spice mixture and olive oil. Spread on a large lined baking tray.
5. Roast vegetables for 20 minutes.
6. Add the spiced lamb steaks to the tray alongside the vegetables.
7. Roast for a further 15-18 minutes until lamb is cooked through and vegetables are golden.
8. Rest the lamb for 5 minutes, then slice.
9. Drizzle with remaining lemon juice and scatter with fresh parsley.
