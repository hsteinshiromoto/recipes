# Baked Salmon with Lemon and Dill

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Salmon Fillets]] - 3 fillets (150g each)
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, sliced into rounds
- [ ] [[Dill]] - 3 tablespoons, chopped
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Broccoli]] - 300g, cut into florets
- [ ] [[Asparagus]] - 200g, trimmed
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Baking tray]]
- [[oven]]

## Instructions
1. Preheat [[oven]] to 200C (400F).
2. Place the [[Salmon Fillets]] on a lined [[Baking tray]].
3. Season with salt, pepper, and minced [[Garlic]].
4. Top each fillet with [[Dill]] and [[Lemon]] slices.
5. Arrange [[Broccoli]] and [[Asparagus]] around the salmon on the tray.
6. Drizzle vegetables lightly with oil, salt, and pepper.
7. Bake for 12-15 minutes until salmon is just cooked through and flakes easily.
8. Serve immediately.
