# Meal Plan 2026-W11

## Weekend Batch Prep (Sunday March 8)

These items are prepared on Sunday to minimize weekday cooking:

1. **Poach chicken breast** (1kg total): Poach in simmering water with ginger, garlic, and a pinch of salt for 15-18 minutes. Shred and divide into 3 portions: Monday lunch (400g), Wednesday lunch (300g), Saturday lunch (300g for soup). Reserve poaching liquid as light broth.
2. **Cook Pumpkin and Lentil Soup** (full recipe, 6 servings): Store in fridge for Monday and Tuesday lunches.
3. **Cook rice** (500g dry): Store in fridge. Used Monday dinner, Tuesday dinner.
4. **Wash and chop vegetables**: Dice onions, mince garlic, grate ginger, cut broccoli florets, slice capsicums. Store in labelled containers.
5. **Hard-boil 6 eggs**: For afternoon snacks across the week.

## Prep Strategy

- **Sunday batch prep (March 8, before the week starts)**:
  - Poach 1kg chicken breast in simmering salted water with ginger and garlic (15-18 min). Shred and portion into 3 containers: 400g (Monday lunch quinoa bowl -- if using), 300g (Wednesday lunch salad), 300g (Sunday soup). Reserve poaching liquid.
  - Cook Pumpkin and Lentil Soup (full recipe, 6 servings). Store in fridge in 2 batches of 3 servings each.
  - Cook 500g dry rice. Cool and refrigerate for Monday and Tuesday dinners.
  - Hard-boil 6 eggs. Store in fridge for Mon/Thu/Sun afternoon snacks.
  - Wash and chop: dice 3 onions, mince garlic (1 head), grate ginger (30g), cut 500g broccoli florets, slice 2 capsicums. Store in labelled containers.
  - Portion out daily snack packs: almonds, walnuts, mixed seeds.

- **Monday evening prep**:
  - No additional prep needed. Rice and soup are from weekend batch.

- **Wednesday evening prep**:
  - Bake sweet potatoes for Wednesday dinner (can put in oven while assembling salad for lunch).
  - Chop vegetables for Thursday and Friday: slice capsicums, dice cucumbers and tomatoes.

- **Thursday evening prep**:
  - Cook 400g dry rice for Thursday dinner (save extra for Friday dinner side).

- **Saturday morning prep**:
  - Cook 400g dry quinoa for Saturday lunch and Sunday dinner.
  - Marinate chicken breast for Saturday lunch bowl while quinoa cooks.
  - Prepare spice rub for Saturday dinner lamb tray bake.

- **Leftover reuse plan**:
  - Sunday batch rice (500g dry) -> Monday dinner Ginger Miso Beef + Tuesday dinner Spicy Prawn Stir Fry
  - Sunday batch chicken (1kg) -> Wednesday lunch Asian Chicken and Kale Salad (300g) + Sunday lunch Silverbeet Soup (300g)
  - Pumpkin and Lentil Soup (6 servings) -> Monday lunch (3 serves) + Tuesday lunch (3 serves)
  - Thursday dinner rice (400g dry batch) -> Thursday Teriyaki Salmon Bowl + Friday dinner Turkey Lettuce Wraps side
  - Saturday quinoa (400g dry batch) -> Saturday lunch Mediterranean Bowl + Sunday dinner Lemon Garlic Prawns
  - Sunday lunch Silverbeet Soup (extra portion) -> Monday W12 lunch
