## Prep Strategy

- **Sunday evening prep (before the week starts)**:
  - Prepare [[Overnight Oats with Protein]] for Monday breakfast (5 min).
  - Hard-boil 6 eggs for snacks throughout the week.
  - Wash and chop vegetables for Monday and Tuesday: dice onions, mince garlic, grate ginger, cut broccoli florets.
  - Portion out snack packs: almonds, walnuts, mixed seeds into daily portions.

- **Monday evening prep**:
  - Cook 400g rice total (extra for Tuesday/Wednesday reuse).
  - Marinate chicken for Tuesday's Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken Salad overnight.

- **Wednesday evening prep**:
  - Prepare [[Overnight Oats with Protein]] for Thursday breakfast.
  - Chop vegetables for Thursday and Friday meals.

- **Friday evening prep**:
  - Marinate chicken for Saturday's Greek Chicken Skewers overnight.

- **Leftover reuse plan**:
  - Monday dinner rice -> Wednesday dinner Lean Beef and Vegetable Stir Fry
  - Tuesday lunch extra chicken -> repurpose for quick wraps or salad if needed
  - Thursday dinner Poached Chicken and Rice Soup -> Friday lunch (full portion saved)
  - Saturday dinner extra tzatziki -> Sunday lunch Quinoa Chicken Power Bowl accompaniment
  - Wednesday lunch extra turkey mince -> snack filling for rice cakes on Friday
