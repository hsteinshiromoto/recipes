## Prep Strategy

- **Sunday evening prep (before the week starts)**:
  - Prepare [[Overnight Oats with Protein]] for Monday breakfast (5 min). Uses Greek yoghurt already on hand.
  - Hard-boil 6 eggs for snacks throughout the week (Wednesday and Sunday).
  - Wash and chop vegetables for Monday and Tuesday: dice onions, mince garlic, grate ginger, cut broccoli florets, slice capsicums.
  - Portion out snack packs: almonds, walnuts, mixed seeds into daily portions.

- **Monday evening prep**:
  - Cook 400g rice total with schnitzel stir fry (extra for Wednesday and Thursday reuse).
  - Marinate chicken in Greek yoghurt mixture for Tuesday's Greek Yoghurt Marinated Chicken with Vegetables (uses yoghurt on hand).

- **Tuesday evening prep**:
  - Set aside leftover sliced chicken from dinner (approx 200g) for Wednesday lunch salad.

- **Wednesday evening prep**:
  - Prepare [[Overnight Oats with Protein]] for Friday breakfast.
  - Chop vegetables for Thursday and Friday meals.

- **Friday evening prep**:
  - Marinate chicken for Saturday's Greek Chicken Skewers overnight.

- **Leftover reuse plan**:
  - Monday dinner rice (400g batch) -> Wednesday dinner Lean Beef and Vegetable Stir Fry + Thursday lunch Poached Chicken and Rice Soup
  - Tuesday dinner extra chicken -> Wednesday lunch Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken Salad (served cold, no re-cooking needed)
  - Thursday lunch Poached Chicken and Rice Soup (extra portion) -> Friday lunch (reheat only)
  - Saturday dinner extra tzatziki -> Sunday lunch Turkey and Black Bean Lettuce Cups (as optional sauce)

- **On-hand items usage**:
  - **Chicken schnitzels**: used Monday lunch (Chicken Schnitzel Salad) and Monday dinner (Chicken Schnitzel Stir Fry) -- fully consumed day 1
  - **Greek yoghurt**: used Monday breakfast (Overnight Oats), Monday lunch (dressing), Monday morning snack, Tuesday dinner (marinade) -- prioritised in first 2 days; remainder purchased fresh for rest of week
