# Turkey and Black Bean Lettuce Cups

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Turkey Mince]] - 500g
- [ ] [[Black Beans]] - 200g (canned, drained and rinsed)
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, finely diced
- [ ] [[Lime]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Cilantro]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 80g (fat-free, as topping)
- [ ] [[Lettuce]] - 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a [[wok]] or large pan over high heat with 1 teaspoon of oil.
2. Add the [[Turkey Mince]] and cook, breaking it up, for 5-6 minutes until browned.
3. Add the [[Garlic]], diced [[Capsicum]], [[Cumin]], and [[Smoked Paprika]]. Cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add the [[Black Beans]] and diced [[Tomato]]. Stir and cook for 3 minutes until heated through.
5. Squeeze [[Lime]] juice over the mixture and stir in [[Cilantro]].
6. Spoon the filling into [[Lettuce]] cups.
7. Top each cup with a small dollop of [[Greek Yoghurt]].
