# Dates with Gorgonzola and Anchovies

*Dátiles, gorgonzola y anchoas.* A no-cook sweet, salty and creamy tapa: soft dates stuffed with gorgonzola, topped with an anchovy fillet, finished with olive oil and chilli flakes.

The video gives no quantities; allow 2–3 dates per person.

![[Dates with Gorgonzola and Anchovies.jpg]]

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Medjool Dates|Medjool dates]]
- [ ] [[Gorgonzola]] (about 1 tsp per date)
- [ ] [[Anchovies|Anchovy fillets]] in oil (1 per date)
- [ ] Extra virgin [[olive oil]]
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes|Chilli flakes]]

## Equipment

## Instructions

1. Slit each date lengthways and remove the pit, keeping the date in one piece.
2. Fill each date with a piece of gorgonzola, pressing it in so it sits slightly proud of the opening.
3. Lay an anchovy fillet over the cheese.
4. Arrange on a plate, drizzle generously with olive oil and sprinkle with chilli flakes.
5. Eat whole, swiping the dates through the oil on the plate.
