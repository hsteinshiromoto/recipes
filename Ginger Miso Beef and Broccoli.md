# Ginger Miso Beef and Broccoli

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Flank Steak]] - 400g, sliced thin against the grain
- [ ] [[Broccoli]] - 500g, cut into florets (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tbsp
- [ ] [[Rice Vinegar]] - 1 tbsp
- [ ] [[Honey]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tbsp
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 3 stalks, sliced for garnish
- [ ] [[Rice]] - 300g dry rice, cooked as per packet

## Equipment

- [[Wok]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Cook rice according to packet directions. Start with 300g dry to produce a batch (extra for reuse).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, garlic, and ginger to make the sauce.
3. Heat a wok over high heat until smoking. Add a tiny amount of oil.
4. Sear the beef slices in batches for 1-2 minutes. Remove and set aside.
5. Add broccoli to the wok with 2 tbsp water. Stir fry for 4 minutes until bright green and tender-crisp.
6. Return beef to the wok. Pour sauce over and toss everything together for 1 minute.
7. Serve over steamed rice. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced scallions.
