# Asian Chicken and Kale Salad

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 450g
- [ ] [[Kale]] - 200g, stems removed and leaves roughly torn (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 2, peeled and julienned
- [ ] [[Cabbage]] - 150g red or napa, shredded
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 3, sliced
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tbsp

### Dressing (adapted from [[Asian Peanut Ginger Dressing]])
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 10g, grated
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 1 clove, grated
- [ ] [[Rice Vinegar]] - 3 tbsp
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tbsp
- [ ] [[Honey]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tsp toasted
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp

## Equipment

- [[Bowl]]
- [[Wok]]

## Instructions

1. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper. Poach in simmering water for 12-15 minutes until cooked through (internal temp 74C). Remove and cool for 5 minutes, then shred with two forks.
2. Massage kale leaves with a pinch of salt for 1 minute until slightly softened.
3. Whisk together all dressing ingredients until combined.
4. In a large bowl, combine kale, carrot, cabbage, and scallions.
5. Add shredded chicken and toss with dressing.
6. Garnish with sesame seeds. Serve immediately.
