# Pumpkin and Lentil Soup

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Pumpkin]] - 800g, peeled and cubed (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Red Lentils]] - 200g, rinsed
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1 large, diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 4 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 2 medium, diced
- [ ] [[Celery]] - 2 stalks, diced
- [ ] [[Chicken Broth]] - 1.5L
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Turmeric]] - 1/2 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Coriander Seed]] - 1 tsp ground
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - pinch
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - small handful, chopped
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tbsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Instant Pot]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook for 3 minutes until softened.
2. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add cumin, turmeric, smoked paprika, coriander, and chilli flakes. Stir and cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add carrot, celery, pumpkin cubes, red lentils, and chicken broth.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 25 minutes until pumpkin and lentils are completely soft.
6. Using a hand blender, partially blend the soup — leave some texture for interest.
7. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
8. Serve topped with fresh parsley. Makes 6 portions.
   - Serves 3 for dinner; reserve 3 portions for next day lunch.
