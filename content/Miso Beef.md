# Miso Beef

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2kg of chuck roast.
- [ ] 1 cup of [[dark soy sauce]].
- [ ] 2 tbsp of miso.
- [ ] Whites of 2 bunches of [[scallions]].
- [ ] Crushed [[Garlic|garlic]] cloves.
- [ ] Crushed [[Ginger]].
- [ ] 1 cup of Chinese cooking wine.

## Equipment

- [ ] [[Pressure cooker]].

## Instructions

1. Separate the [[scallion]] whites from the greens and cut into slices.
2. Saute the [[scallions]], the [[garlic]] and the [[ginger]].
3. Cut the chuck roast in cubes.
4. Add the Chinese cooking wine to deglaze the pot, before adding the chuck roast.
5. Mix the [[dark soy sauce]] with the [[miso]].
5. Add the chuck roast and the mixture to the [[pressure cooker]] and cook with high pressure for 45 minutes.

Use the greens parts of the [[scallions]] to serve.
Serve with [[rice]].
