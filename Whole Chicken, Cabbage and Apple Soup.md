# Whole Chicken, Cabbage and Apple Soup

A deliberately simple soup: a whole chicken is pressure-cooked low and slow on a bed of [[Napa Cabbage|cabbage]], [[Onion|onion]] and [[Apple|apple]], producing a sweet, concentrated broth with no need for extra aromatics.

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 large [[Whole Chicken|whole chicken]]
- [ ] 1/2 head [[Napa Cabbage|napa cabbage]], roughly chopped
- [ ] 1 [[Onion]], sliced
- [ ] 1 [[Apple]], sliced
- [ ] Salt, to taste

## Equipment
- [ ] Pressure cooker or large stock pot with lid

## Instructions

1. Line the bottom of the pressure cooker with the napa cabbage, onion and apple.
2. Season with salt.
3. Place the whole chicken on top of the vegetables and fruit.
4. Cover and cook on low pressure until the chicken is fully cooked through and the vegetables have broken down into the broth.
5. Ladle the concentrated broth over the chicken and cabbage to serve.
