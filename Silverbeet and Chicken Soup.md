# Silverbeet and Chicken Soup

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 400g
- [ ] [[Silverbeet]] - 200g, stems removed and leaves roughly chopped (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[White Beans]] - 1 x 400g can, drained and rinsed
- [ ] [[Chicken Broth]] - 1.2L
- [ ] [[Leek]] - 1, white and pale green parts, sliced (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Celery]] - 2 stalks, diced
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Parsley]] - handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - pinch
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Instant Pot]]
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add leek, celery, and carrot. Cook for 4 minutes until softened.
2. Add garlic and chilli flakes, cook for 1 minute.
3. Add whole chicken breasts and chicken broth. Bring to a gentle boil.
4. Reduce heat and simmer for 15-18 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
5. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, and return to the pot.
6. Add white beans and silverbeet. Simmer for 5 minutes until silverbeet is wilted and beans are heated through.
7. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
8. Serve topped with fresh parsley. Makes 4 portions; save 1 extra for next-day lunch.
