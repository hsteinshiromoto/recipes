# Banana Nut Bread

![[Banana Nut Bread.jpg]]

## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 large (250 g) ripe [[Banana|bananas]]
- [ ] 90 mL (6 tbsp) [[butter]]
- [ ] 5 mL (1 tsp) [[Vanilla Extract|vanilla extract]]
- [ ] 2 large [[Egg|eggs]]
- [ ] 167 g (1 1/3 cups) all-purpose flour
- [ ] 130 g (2/3 cup) sugar
- [ ] 2.3 g (1/2 tsp) [[baking soda]]
- [ ] 1.2 g (1/4 tsp) [[baking powder]]
- [ ] 3 g (1/2 tsp) salt
- [ ] 70 g (1/2 cup) chopped [[Walnuts|walnuts]]

## Equipment
- [[oven]]
- Loaf pan

## Instructions
1. Butter and flour a loaf pan.
2. Preheat the oven to 170°C (350°F).
3. Mash the bananas. Melt the butter. Lightly beat the eggs.
4. Mash the bananas, butter, vanilla and eggs together until smooth.
5. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking soda, baking powder and salt together.
6. Fold the dry ingredients and the walnuts into the banana mixture.
7. Bake at 170°C (350°F) for 55 min.
8. Cool 10 min in the pan, then cool on a wire rack.
