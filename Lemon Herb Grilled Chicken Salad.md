# Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken Salad

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 400g
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 2, juiced and zested
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[oregano]] - 1 teaspoon dried
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2 medium, diced
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2 red, thinly sliced
- [ ] [[Fetta]] - 40g, crumbled
- [ ] [[Arugula]] - 100g
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Baking tray]]

## Instructions
1. Butterfly or pound the [[Chicken Breast]] to even thickness.
2. Marinate with half the [[Lemon]] juice, [[Garlic]], [[oregano]], salt, and pepper for at least 10 minutes.
3. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Cook the chicken for 5-6 minutes per side until cooked through (internal temp 74C).
5. Let rest for 3 minutes, then slice.
6. In a large bowl, combine the [[Arugula]], diced [[Cucumber]], [[Tomato]], and sliced [[Onion]].
7. Dress with the remaining [[Lemon]] juice, a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper.
8. Top with sliced chicken and crumbled [[Fetta]].
