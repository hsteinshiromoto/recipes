# Nuoc Cham

Vietnamese fish sauce dipping sauce. The "golden ratio" is **1:1:5**: equal parts fish sauce and sugar, and five times that amount of water.

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1/4 cup [[Fish Sauce|fish sauce]]
- [ ] 1/4 cup sugar
- [ ] 1 1/4 cups filtered water
- [ ] 1 tbsp minced [[Garlic|garlic]]
- [ ] 3 [[Thai Chilli|Thai chillies]], chopped
- [ ] 1 [[Lime|lime]], juiced

## Equipment

## Instructions

1. Combine the fish sauce, sugar and water, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
2. Add the garlic, chopped chilli and lime juice.
3. Store in the fridge for 3–4 weeks.

### Variations

- Substitute the water with coconut water, Coco Rico, or even Sprite.
- Swap the lime for vinegar (e.g. [[Rice Vinegar|rice vinegar]]).
