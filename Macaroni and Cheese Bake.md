# Macaroni and Cheese Bake

![[Macaroni and Cheese Bake.jpg]]

## Ingredients

### Boiled Macaroni
- [ ] 4 L (4 qt) water
- [ ] 18 g (1 tbsp) table salt
- [ ] 450 g (1 lb) [[Elbow Macaroni|elbow macaroni]]

### Cheese Sauce
- [ ] 55 g (4 tbsp) melted [[butter]]
- [ ] 45 g (5 tbsp) all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 L (3 × 12 oz cans) [[Evaporated Milk|evaporated milk]]
- [ ] 0.3 g (1/8 tsp) ground [[Nutmeg|nutmeg]]
- [ ] 3.3 g (1 tsp) ground mustard seed
- [ ] 12 g (2 tsp) table salt
- [ ] 5 mL (1 tsp) hot sauce
- [ ] 140 g (5 oz) American cheese
- [ ] 225 g (8 oz) extra sharp [[Cheddar|cheddar cheese]]
- [ ] 85 g (3 oz) Monterey Jack cheese
- [ ] 1/2 cup reserved pasta water

### Topping
- [ ] 4 slices white sandwich bread
- [ ] 55 g (4 tbsp) melted [[butter]]
- [ ] 35 g (1/4 cup) grated [[Parmiggiano|Parmesan cheese]]

## Equipment
- [[oven]]
- 9×13 in baking pan
- Food processor

## Instructions

### Boiled Macaroni
1. Boil the water and dissolve the salt in it.
2. Add the macaroni and boil until al dente.
3. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water, then drain and rinse the macaroni.

### Bake
1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F).
2. Cook the melted butter and flour, stirring, until light brown (1 min).
3. Slowly stir in the evaporated milk.
4. Stir in the nutmeg, mustard, salt and hot sauce.
5. Remove from the heat when thickened (4 min).
6. Grate the American, cheddar and Monterey Jack cheeses and stir them in until melted.
7. Stir in the reserved pasta water and the cooked macaroni until coated.
8. Pour into a 9×13 in pan.
9. Pulse the bread, melted butter and Parmesan into breadcrumbs and scatter over the top.
10. Bake 25 min at 175°C (350°F).
11. Let rest 10 min before serving.
