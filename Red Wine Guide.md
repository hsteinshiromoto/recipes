# Red Wine Guide

Red wine varieties mapped by body (light-bodied: light, delicate, higher acidity → full-bodied: rich, bold, higher tannin) and flavour profile (fruity: bright red fruit, berry, cherry → savory: earthy, herbal, spice).

![[Find Your Red Wine.jpg]]

## Quadrants

| | Light-bodied | Full-bodied |
|---|---|---|
| **Fruity** | Pinot Noir, Gamay, Barbera, Zweigelt | Merlot, Malbec, Grenache (Garnacha), Cabernet Sauvignon, Australian Shiraz |
| **Savory** | Sangiovese, Carménère, Tempranillo, Nebbiolo | Northern Rhône Syrah, Petite Sirah, Aglianico, Zinfandel (Old Vine), Montepulciano d'Abruzzo, Monastrell (Mourvèdre), Rioja (Reserva) |
