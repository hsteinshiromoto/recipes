# Grilled Fish Tacos with Cabbage Slaw

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[White Fish Fillets]] - 400g (cod, barramundi, or similar)
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Garlic Power]] - 1/2 teaspoon
- [ ] [[Lime]] - 2, juiced
- [ ] [[Cabbage]] - 200g, finely shredded
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 1, julienned
- [ ] [[Cilantro]] - a handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 100g (fat-free)
- [ ] [[Tortilla]] - 6 small corn tortillas
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Baking tray]]

## Instructions
1. Season the [[White Fish Fillets]] with [[Cumin]], [[Smoked Paprika]], [[Garlic Power]], half the [[Lime]] juice, salt, and pepper.
2. Heat a non-stick pan or grill pan over medium-high heat.
3. Cook the fish for 3-4 minutes per side until flaky and cooked through.
4. Meanwhile, toss the shredded [[Cabbage]], julienned [[Carrot]], and [[Cilantro]] with the remaining [[Lime]] juice and a pinch of salt.
5. Mix the [[Greek Yoghurt]] with a squeeze of [[Lime]], salt, and pepper for the crema.
6. Warm the [[Tortilla|tortillas]] in a dry pan.
7. Break the fish into chunks and divide among the tortillas.
8. Top with cabbage slaw and yoghurt crema.
