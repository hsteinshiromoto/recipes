# Chicken Schnitzel and Vegetable Stir Fry

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Chicken Schnitzel]] - 400g (pre-made, sliced into strips)
- [ ] [[Broccoli]] - 200g, cut into small florets
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1, sliced
- [ ] [[Carrot]] - 1, sliced thin on the bias
- [ ] [[Snow Peas]] - 100g
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Ginger]] - 2cm knob, grated
- [ ] [[light soy sauce]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Oyster Sauce]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[Sesame Seeds]] - 1 tablespoon
- [ ] [[scallion]] - 2, sliced
- [ ] [[Rice]] - to serve

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Cook [[Rice]] according to preference (or use Instant Pot). Make extra (400g total) for leftover use later in the week.
2. Heat a [[wok]] over high heat with 1 teaspoon of oil.
3. Add the [[Broccoli]], [[Capsicum]], [[Carrot]], and [[Snow Peas]]. Stir fry for 3-4 minutes until tender-crisp.
4. Add [[Garlic]] and [[Ginger]], cook for 30 seconds.
5. Add the sliced [[Chicken Schnitzel]] strips and toss for 2 minutes until heated through.
6. Add [[light soy sauce]] and [[Oyster Sauce]], toss everything together for 1 minute.
7. Garnish with [[Sesame Seeds]] and sliced [[scallion]].
8. Serve over steamed [[Rice]].
