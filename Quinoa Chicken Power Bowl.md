# Quinoa Chicken Power Bowl

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Quinoa]] - 200g
- [ ] [[Chicken Breast]] - 400g
- [ ] [[Kale]] - 100g, stems removed, chopped
- [ ] [[Chickpeas]] - 200g (canned, drained)
- [ ] [[Cucumber]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 2, diced
- [ ] [[Lemon]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Tahini]] - 2 tablespoons
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 1 clove, minced
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[Bowl]]

## Instructions
1. Cook the [[Quinoa]] according to package directions (or use sous vide at 80C for 1h). Let cool slightly.
2. Season [[Chicken Breast]] with salt, pepper, and a squeeze of [[Lemon]].
3. Grill or pan-fry the chicken over medium-high heat for 6-7 minutes per side. Let rest, then slice.
4. Massage the [[Kale]] with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of [[Lemon]] until slightly wilted.
5. Make the dressing: whisk [[Tahini]], remaining [[Lemon]] juice, [[Garlic]], 2 tablespoons water, salt, and pepper.
6. Assemble bowls with [[Quinoa]], [[Kale]], [[Chickpeas]], [[Cucumber]], [[Tomato]], and sliced chicken.
7. Drizzle with tahini dressing.
