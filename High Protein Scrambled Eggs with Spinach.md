# High Protein Scrambled Eggs with Spinach

## Ingredients
- [ ] [[Egg]] - 6 large
- [ ] [[Egg]] - 4 whites only
- [ ] [[Spinach]] - 100g
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 2 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Tomato]] - 1 medium, diced
- [ ] [[Fetta]] - 30g, crumbled
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1/2 small, diced
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment
- [[wok]]

## Instructions
1. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat with a light spray of oil.
2. Saute the diced [[Onion]] and minced [[Garlic]] for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the [[Spinach]] and cook until wilted, about 2 minutes.
4. Add the diced [[Tomato]] and cook for 1 minute.
5. Whisk together the whole [[Egg|eggs]] and egg whites. Pour into the pan.
6. Stir gently with a spatula, folding the eggs as they set, about 3-4 minutes.
7. Remove from heat while still slightly wet (they will continue cooking).
8. Top with crumbled [[Fetta]] and season with salt and pepper.
