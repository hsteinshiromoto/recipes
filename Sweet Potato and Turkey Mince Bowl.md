# Sweet Potato and Turkey Mince Bowl

## Ingredients

- [ ] [[Sweet Potato]] - 600g, peeled and cubed (autumn seasonal)
- [ ] [[Turkey Mince]] - 450g
- [ ] [[Black Beans]] - 1 x 400g can, drained and rinsed
- [ ] [[Onion]] - 1, diced
- [ ] [[Garlic]] - 3 cloves, minced
- [ ] [[Capsicum]] - 1 red, diced
- [ ] [[Cumin]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Smoked Paprika]] - 1 tsp
- [ ] [[Chilli Flakes]] - 1/4 tsp
- [ ] [[Tomato Paste]] - 2 tbsp
- [ ] [[Chicken Broth]] - 100ml
- [ ] [[Lime]] - 1, juiced
- [ ] [[Cilantro]] - handful, chopped
- [ ] [[Greek Yoghurt]] - 3 tbsp fat-free, for serving
- [ ] Olive oil - 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Equipment

- [[Wok]]
- [[Baking tray]]
- [[Oven]]

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200C. Toss sweet potato cubes with a light spray of oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking tray and roast for 25 minutes until golden and tender.
2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Cook onion for 3 minutes.
3. Add garlic, capsicum, cumin, smoked paprika, and chilli flakes. Cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add turkey mince. Break up and cook for 5-6 minutes until browned.
5. Stir in tomato paste, chicken broth, and black beans. Simmer for 5 minutes until sauce thickens.
6. Season with lime juice, salt, and pepper.
7. Serve turkey mince and black beans over roasted sweet potato. Top with a dollop of Greek yoghurt and fresh cilantro.
