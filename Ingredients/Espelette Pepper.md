# Piment d'Espelette

Grown in the French Basque Country, this Gorria chilli is dried and ground into a fragrant red powder. It adds gentle heat to soups, grilled meat and fish.

- **Culinary use:** Seasoning meat & fish
- **Dried form:** Whole & ground
