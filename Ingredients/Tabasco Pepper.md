# Tabasco

Best known for its use in Louisiana's Tabasco sauce, this small chilli ripens from yellow through orange to red. It brings sharp heat to pepper sauces.

- **Culinary use:** Hot sauces
- **Ripening:** Yellow → Orange → Red
