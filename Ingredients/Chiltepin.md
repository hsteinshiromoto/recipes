# Chiltepin

This tiny wild chilli grows in northern Mexico and the southwestern United States. It is eaten fresh, pickled or dried, and crushed into salsas and seasonings.

- **Culinary use:** Salsas & seasoning
- **Common forms:** Fresh, dried & pickled
