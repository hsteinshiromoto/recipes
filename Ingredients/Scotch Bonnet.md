# Scotch Bonnet

A staple of Jamaican cooking, Scotch Bonnet brings strong heat and a fruity flavor. It is a familiar ingredient in jerk seasoning, sauces and marinades.

- **Culinary use:** Jerk seasoning & sauces
- **Distinctive shape:** Bonnet-shaped
