# Rocoto

An Andean chilli with thick, juicy flesh and dark seeds, rocoto is used in sauces and stuffed dishes. In Arequipa, it is the main ingredient in rocoto relleno.

- **Culinary use:** Stuffing & sauces
- **Distinctive feature:** Dark seeds
