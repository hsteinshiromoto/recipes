# Hungarian Hot Wax

Named for its waxy skin, this Hungarian chilli is often harvested pale yellow. It turns orange and then red as it ripens, and is used fresh, stuffed or pickled.

- **Culinary use:** Pickling & stuffing
- **Ripening:** Yellow → Orange → Red
