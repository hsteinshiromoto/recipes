# Ají Amarillo

A staple of Peruvian cooking, ají amarillo brings fruity heat and a rich orange color to sauces and stews. Its name means yellow chilli, but the ripe fruit is orange.

- **Culinary use:** Sauces & stews
- **Dried form:** Ají Mirasol
