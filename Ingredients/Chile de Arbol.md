# Chile de Árbol

Named for its woody stems, chile de árbol is used fresh or dried in salsas, soups and hot sauces. Its slender pods turn bright red when ripe.

- **Culinary use:** Salsas & hot sauces
- **Dried form:** Chile de Árbol
