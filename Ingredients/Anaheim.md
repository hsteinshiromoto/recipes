# Anaheim

Originating in New Mexico, this mild chilli takes its name from Anaheim, California. It is often roasted and peeled before being used in stuffed dishes, sauces and stews.

- **Culinary use:** Stuffing & stews
- **Preparation:** Roasted & peeled
