# Bhut Jolokia

Also known as ghost pepper, bhut jolokia is grown in northeastern India. Its extreme heat and fruity flavor make small amounts enough for chilli sauces and pickles.

- **Culinary use:** Sauces & pickles
- **Also known as:** Ghost Pepper
