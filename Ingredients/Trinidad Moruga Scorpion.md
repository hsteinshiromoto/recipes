# Trinidad Moruga Scorpion

Named after Moruga in Trinidad, this deeply wrinkled chilli combines fruity flavor with extreme heat. It is used in very small quantities in hot sauces.

- **Culinary use:** Hot sauces
- **Flavor:** Fruity & sweet
