# Chinese Bolognese

A Cantonese take on bolognese: ground beef is browned hard, a finely diced "Hong Kong sofrito" of [[Ham|ham]], [[Carrot|carrot]], [[Onion|onion]], [[Celery|celery]] and spring onion is cooked separately to keep its texture, then both are combined with [[Tomato Puree|tomato puree]] and red wine and simmered low for depth of colour and flavour.

## Ingredients
- [ ] 500g ground [[Beef Mince|beef]]
- [ ] 150g [[Ham]], finely diced
- [ ] 1 [[Carrot]], finely diced
- [ ] 1 [[Onion]], finely diced
- [ ] 2 stalks [[Celery]], finely diced
- [ ] 2 spring onions, finely diced
- [ ] 2 tbsp [[Tomato Puree|tomato puree]]
- [ ] 1 cup red wine
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] Salt, to taste

## Equipment
- [ ] [[wok]] or heavy-bottomed pot

## Instructions

1. In a pot with a little olive oil, brown the ground beef hard over high heat until deeply coloured — don't rush this step.
2. Add the tomato puree to the browned beef and stir through.
3. Separately, sauté the diced ham, carrot, onion, celery and spring onion ("Hong Kong sofrito") until about 80% cooked, keeping the dice distinct rather than mushing it down.
4. Add the red wine to the beef to deglaze the pan, scraping up the browned bits for colour and flavour.
5. Once combined, fold the sofrito back into the beef mixture.
6. Season with a sprinkling of salt to taste.
7. Simmer on low for as long as you can to reduce and concentrate the sauce.
8. Serve hot over pasta.
